BDT Connect / Information

Consultation terms

BDT Connect is the AI consultation and workflow implementation service of BDT Talent Group. This website is a starting point for discussing your needs.

Sending an inquiry

Submitting the form asks BDT Talent Group to contact you about your request. It does not book a session, start a subscription, authorize a charge, or begin paid work.

Agreeing on a project

The service scope, deliverables, fees, timing, and any third-party software costs are discussed before paid work begins. Project-specific terms will be provided separately.

Examples on this site

The workflow examples illustrate possible uses of AI. The right approach depends on your process, tools, and requirements. Examples are not promises of a particular result.

Access and review

Please do not send passwords or confidential client information in your initial inquiry. We will discuss any necessary access and review steps as part of scoping a project.

Contact

For questions about a consultation or proposed project, use the contact form .
