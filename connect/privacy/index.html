BDT Connect / Information

Privacy

This page describes the information collected through the BDT Connect consultation website. BDT Connect is a service of BDT Talent Group.

Information you choose to send

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, area of interest, and a description of the work you want help with. You may also provide a business or project name and the tools you use.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or confidential client information. Any access needed for a later project should be discussed separately.

How your inquiry is handled

Your form submission is sent through Formspree to BDT Talent Group so we can review your request and respond about consultation services. Formspree and the website hosting provider may process technical request information as part of delivering and protecting their services.

For details about the form provider’s practices, see Formspree’s privacy policy .

Payments and accounts

This consultation website does not ask you to create an account or enter payment details. If a project later requires a separate service or payment process, its details will be provided at that stage.

Questions about your information

To ask about information you have sent, or request a correction or deletion, use the contact form and include “Privacy inquiry” in your message.
