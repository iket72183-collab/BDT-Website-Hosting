A few things to know

Good questions.
Straight answers.

You do not need to be technical to start. You just need to know what you want to make easier.

I know I need AI. Where do I start?

Start with one task that takes too much time. Tell us what happens today and what you want to change. You do not need to know which model, agent, or automation tool to use.

What happens during a consultation?

We talk through your current workflow, the tools involved, and the result you want. From there, we recommend a practical next step and discuss whether implementation makes sense.

Can you build and set up the workflow too?

Yes. After we agree on the scope, implementation can include agent configuration, app connections, testing, documentation, and a walkthrough. The specific deliverables are agreed for your project.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the scope. After your inquiry, we discuss your needs and agree on the consultation or project fee before paid work begins. We also discuss any third-party software or usage costs. Sending an inquiry does not commit you to paid work.

Do I need a subscription or a new app?

You can start by sending an inquiry through this site. There is no subscription signup or app download in this consultation flow. We will discuss the tools your project needs with you.

Can we keep a person in the loop?

Yes. We design review and approval steps around the task. AI can make mistakes, so testing, clear access limits, and human review are part of planning the workflow.

Do you work remotely or in person?

BDT Talent Group is based in Houston. Tell us your location and whether you prefer remote or on-site help; we will discuss what works for your project.
