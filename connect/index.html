Before we talk

A few good questions.

Do I need to know which AI tools to use?

No. Start with the task you want to improve. We help you find a practical approach for your tools, team, and budget.

Can you build it and train my team?

Yes. An agreed project can include agent setup, app connections, testing, documentation, and a team walkthrough. We plan human review and approval steps around the task.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the scope. We agree on fees and discuss any software costs before paid work begins. An inquiry does not book a session or commit you to paid work.

Do you work remotely or in person?

We’re based in Houston. Tell us where you are and whether you prefer remote or on-site help; we’ll discuss what works for your project.
